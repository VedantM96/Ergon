04 THE CLARITY MODEL

A transparent score,
not a black box.

Four visible rules estimate how much clarification a potential commitment needs. Each one contributes a named number of vagueness points.

Ergon's Review displays the inverse clarity value (100 − vagueness): Clear at 85+, Fuzzy at 55–84, and Vague below 55. These are the same bands expressed in the app's clarity scale.

05 DATA & PRIVACY

Clear about what
happens to text.

A factual account of the current app—not a broader privacy promise.

01 / ANALYSIS

AI, with a fallback

Transcript text is saved to this workspace. When available, Ergon submits it to the configured AI service for analysis. If the response is unavailable or unusable, a rules-only parser can produce suggestions on the server.

02 / REDACTION

No built-in masking

The current app has no redaction control. Remove sensitive information yourself before submitting any transcript you are not permitted to share.

03 / OFFLINE

Not an offline mode

Ergon requires a network connection. The rules-only fallback still runs on the server and does not make transcript analysis available offline.

What can I upload? +

Ergon accepts .txt, .vtt, and .srt files up to 1 MB, or pasted text. The transcript must be between 40 and 60,000 characters.

Does Ergon create tasks automatically? +

No. Suggested commitments remain in Review until a person edits or confirms them. Only confirmed tasks appear on the Board.

Does it create Jira tickets or send messages? +

No external ticket integration is connected. The Dashboard can prepare reminder links, but Ergon does not send messages. Jira and Slack are future directions.

HELP CENTER

How do I start a review?

Open Review, paste meeting text with speaker names or choose a .txt, .vtt, or .srt file, then select Review commitments. The file is analyzed and verified directly in your workspace.

What does the vague score mean?

It is a transparent SMART-gap heuristic. It evaluates missing owners (+30), unspecific deadlines (+30), vague deliverables (+20), and hedge phrases like "maybe" or "look into" (+20). Lower is clearer.

How does Ergon process transcripts and save profiles?

Ergon analyzes transcripts using SMART commitment heuristics and natural language patterns to detect missing owners, vague deadlines, and unspecific deliverables. All reviewed meetings, clarified tasks, and team rosters are automatically saved to your selected workspace profile.

How do reminders and Google Calendar integration work?

Approve a task and enter a due date to create a Google Calendar draft link with prefilled owner and deliverable details. Reminder text can also be copied directly to your clipboard.

Where can I view tasks and export data?

Approved promises appear on the Board. Use the move buttons to update To Do, Doing, and Done. You can export task CSVs at any time or download full JSON workspace backups from Settings.
